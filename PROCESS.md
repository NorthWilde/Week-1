# Process
The starting point of this assignment was the question: why should designers learn programming when AI can already generate code from natural-language instructions?

Before developing my reflection, I watched Dylan Beattie's The Art of Code. The video helped me think about programming from a different perspective. This became an important starting point for my reflection. After watching Dylan Beattie's The Art of Code, I became more interested in the idea of programming as a creative medium. I decided to focus my reflection on the relationship between design, programming, creativity, and AI. I also connected the topic to my own experience with Digital Media Art and interactive tools such as Unity, Unreal Engine, Blender, and Figma.

I used AI mainly for brainstorming, organising my ideas, and improving my English expression. I asked AI to suggest possible arguments and essay structures, and then reviewed the suggestions myself. I kept the ideas about programming as a creative medium, programming helping designers understand interactive systems, and AI being a tool that can assist rather than replace designers.
